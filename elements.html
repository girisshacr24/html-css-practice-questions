Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology that enables computers and machines to learn, think, and perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. It is used in areas like healthcare, education, transportation, entertainment, and everyday applications to make work faster and smarter.

AI is becoming an important part of modern life, helping people solve problems, automate tasks, and make better decisions. From voice assistants and recommendation systems to self-driving vehicles and smart healthcare, AI is continuously changing the way we live and work.
