Labrador Retriever

The Labrador Retriever is one of the most popular and friendly dog breeds in the world. Labradors are known for their intelligence, loyalty, and playful nature. They are excellent family dogs and enjoy spending time with people.

Labradors are active dogs that need regular exercise and playtime. They are also easy to train because they are intelligent and eager to learn. Their friendly nature makes them suitable as family pets, guide dogs, and assistance dogs.
